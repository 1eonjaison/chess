CHESS GAME - HELP

GAME MODES

1. Registered Player - Play against another account. Elo changes.
2. Guest - Play against a guest. Elo does not change.
3. Computer - Play against Easy, Medium or Hard AI. Elo does not change.

CHESS PIECES

K - King
Q - Queen
R - Rook
B - Bishop
N - Knight
P - Pawn

Uppercase = White pieces.
Lowercase = Black pieces.
. = Empty square.

BOARD LETTERING

Columns: a to h (left to right).
Rows: 1 to 8 (bottom to top).

Example: e2 means column e, row 2.

HOW TO MOVE

Enter the starting square followed by the ending square.

e2e4 - Move from e2 to e4.
g1f3 - Move from g1 to f3.
e1g1 - Castle kingside.
e7e8q - Promote a pawn to a queen.

PROMOTION LETTERS

q - Queen
r - Rook
b - Bishop
n - Knight

GAME COMMANDS

moves   - Show legal moves.
history - Show previous moves.
undo    - Undo moves.
restart - Restart the game.
claim   - Claim an available draw.
resign  - Resign the game.
help    - Show help.
exit    - Return to menu (resigns in rated games).

GAME RULES

Check - Your king is under attack.
Checkmate - Your king cannot escape check.
Stalemate - No legal moves, but not in check.
Castling - Special move involving the king and rook.
En Passant - Special pawn capture.
Promotion - Replace a pawn reaching the final row.

You win by checkmate or resignation.
Games can end in a draw.

ELO AND LEADERBOARD

New accounts start at 1000 Elo.
Registered matches affect Elo (K = 32).
Guest and computer matches do not affect Elo.
All completed games update statistics.
The leaderboard ranks players by Elo.
